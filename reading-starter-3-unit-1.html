Part A · Reading Check

Read each question. Choose the best answer.

Part B · Language Focus

Practice useful language from the unit.

Part C · Wh- Questions

Answer in complete English sentences. The example answers appear after you check Part A & B.

Example: Because he needs to help his mom prepare for the party.
Example: They need to decorate the room with balloons and get the table ready.
Sample idea: I would put out cups and plates, and then I would decorate the room. (Your own idea is welcome!)

Part D · Mini Writing

Imagine you are getting ready for a celebration. Write 3–5 sentences (about 30 words or more). Include could or would like to, and at least one sequencing word: first, then, or finally.
