Part 1 — TRACE Reading Review

Reading 1

Dogs began living with people thousands of years ago. Over time, we changed them in various ways by breeding them to suit our needs. As a result, dogs come in many types, shapes, and sizes.

Some are small enough to fit in your bag, while others are large, powerful, and heavy. These different types were bred for special purposes, and their shapes tell their stories.

1. When did dogs begin living with people?

2. Why did people breed dogs in different ways?

3. Choose the correct sentence.

Reading 2

In the past, some dogs had special purposes like herding or hunting. Their purposes required characteristics such as being strong, fast and smart.

Clever, fast dogs that can turn very quickly make good shepherds. They can outrun and outthink sheep and cattle.

4. What three characteristics were important for herding or hunting dogs?

5. Clever, fast dogs can make good ______.

Reading 3

Smaller dogs with shorter legs had different purposes. They were much better for going underground or in small places.

Today, small dogs look cute and helpless, but in the past, they were not thought of in this way. They were useful for hunting rats and other small animals.

6. What kind of dogs were useful for going underground?

Reading 4

Today, we also have dogs trained to help people, like police dogs or dogs for blind people.

Police dogs need to be strong and fast in order to help police in dangerous situations. However, dogs for blind people need to be smart because they help people to get around.

Mostly though, people don't think much about a dog's characteristics. They just get a dog because they want to have a pet.

7. What do police dogs need to be?

8. Why do dogs for blind people need to be smart?

9. According to the passage, why do most people get a dog today?
